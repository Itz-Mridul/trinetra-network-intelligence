Simple examples

Example 1: Phone signal −108 dBm and download 2 Mbps: move near a window or outside. If it improves, the location or building is blocking the signal.

Example 2: Phone signal −80 dBm, router response 15 ms, but download 2 Mbps: the signal is fine; the carrier may be busy or its backhaul may be slow.

Example 3: Wi‑Fi −82 dBm and router response 120 ms, while Pi Internet is healthy: move the Wi‑Fi sensor/router or change the Wi‑Fi channel.

Example 4: Router response is healthy but all HTTPS targets fail: the outside carrier/ISP path is down. Do not replace the local router first.

What is Trinetra?

A portable network diagnostic system. It replaces guessing with evidence.

When the connection fails, Trinetra tells you why.

Carry ESP32 sensors and a Raspberry Pi to the site under test. The Pi collects reports, compares the layers, and turns the results into this live dashboard.

📡 Wi-Fi signal

RSSI is signal strength. Numbers closer to zero are stronger: −30 to −50 dBm is excellent; below −75 dBm is a likely dead zone.

🌐 Gateway RTT

Round-trip time to the local gateway. High or unstable RTT usually points to local congestion or interference.

🍓 Pi loopback

Checks whether the sensor can reach the Raspberry Pi hub. This separates a local network problem from an outside ISP problem.

📉 Packet loss

Lost packets create buffering, glitches, and dropped calls. Zero is ideal; sustained loss above 10% is serious.

🩺 Four-layer checklist

  • RF: is the Wi-Fi radio healthy?
  • Gateway: is the router responding?
  • Device: is the sensor healthy?
  • WAN: can the outside Internet be reached?
